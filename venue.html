How to Reach

✈️

By Air

Nearest airport is Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport, Amritsar (~80 km from campus). Prepaid taxis and app-cabs are available to NIT Jalandhar.

🚆

By Train

Jalandhar City Railway Station is ~12 km and Jalandhar Cantt Railway Station is ~18 km from campus. Autos, cabs and buses are readily available.

🚌

By Bus

Jalandhar ISBT (bus stand) is ~15 km from the institute, with interstate bus services connecting major cities of Punjab and neighbouring states.

🚗

By Road

NIT Jalandhar sits on the G.T. Road (NH-1), Amritsar Bypass, well connected by road to Delhi, Chandigarh and Amritsar.

Nearby Hotels

  • Golden Tulip Jalandhar (GT Bypass Road, ~10 min from railway station)
  • WJ Grand Hotel, GT Road, Maqsudan
  • Best Western Plus Jalandhar
  • Park Inn by Radisson Jalandhar
  • Ramada by Wyndham Jalandhar City Centre

Visit the Accommodation page for group booking rates and on-campus stay options for delegates.

Getting Around Campus

  • Located a half-km walk from the Bidhipur railway crossing on the GT Road bypass
  • Well-marked entrance and internal roads connecting the venue, guest houses and hostels
  • On-campus signage and volunteer help desks during the conference days
  • Registration desk and technical session halls within the Department of Electrical Engineering
