Paper Submission – IIPESS-2027

Submission Guidelines

All submissions must strictly follow the IEEE format. Authors can download the manuscript template here.

Prospective authors are invited to submit original and unpublished papers using Microsoft CMT via the link below:

Submit via Microsoft CMT →
🛡️
T1

Power System Protection, Operation, Control, Security

Protection schemes, operational strategies, control methods, and security measures for reliable power system performance.

🔌
T2

Advanced Power Electronics and Drives

Converters, inverters, motor drives, and control strategies for industrial and grid-connected power electronic applications.

🔋
T3

Energy Storage Technologies, Electric Vehicles, and Future Transportation

Battery and storage technologies, EV powertrains, and the evolving landscape of sustainable transportation systems.

🌐
T4

Advancements in Smart Grids, Microgrids, and Nano-grids

Smart grid architectures, distributed microgrid and nano-grid design, and intelligent grid coordination technologies.

📡
T5

Signal Processing and Instrumentation for Power and Energy Systems

Signal processing techniques and instrumentation methods used for monitoring and analysis of power and energy systems.

🤖
T6

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Data-driven Applications for Power and Energy Systems

Machine learning, AI, and data-driven approaches applied to forecasting, optimization, and management of energy systems.

⚡
T7

Recent Developments in High Voltage Engineering

Insulation technologies, high voltage testing, and emerging developments in high voltage power system engineering.

🌱
T8

Sustainable and Renewable Energy Technologies

Solar, wind, hydro, and other renewable technologies, along with sustainability practices across the energy sector.

Note: Complete track details will be updated soon.

⚠ Page length should not exceed 6 pages (IEEE format).

Plagiarism Policy: The conference follows stringent policies for plagiarism as laid by IEEE. Papers having similarities beyond a specified limit of 30% (with References) will be straightaway rejected. Similarity must be below 30% for camera-ready papers. Authors also need to obtain permission from the copyright holder for any third-party content use.
