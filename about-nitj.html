About the Institute

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar is among the 31 NITs established by the Ministry of Education (earlier MHRD), Government of India. The Institute came into existence in 1987 (earlier known as Regional Engineering College, Jalandhar) and was conferred the status of "Institute of National Importance" by an Act of Parliament in 2007.

The institute offers B.Tech., M.Sc., M.Tech., and Ph.D. programmes across a wide range of disciplines, including Biotechnology, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Industrial and Production Engineering, Information Technology, Instrumentation and Control Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Textile Technology.

Campus & Surroundings

The campus sits on a sprawling, green stretch of land along the Jalandhar–Amritsar Highway in Punjab, offering a calm, well-connected setting for conference attendees. Jalandhar itself is centrally located in Punjab, with convenient road and rail links to Amritsar, Chandigarh, and Delhi, making the campus easy to reach for both domestic and international visitors.

The institute provides a vibrant academic environment that fosters innovation, critical thinking, and interdisciplinary research, supported by state-of-the-art infrastructure and modern laboratories — many of which conference attendees will get to experience firsthand during IIPESS 2027.

Research & Innovation Focus

With a strong focus on research, innovation, and entrepreneurship, NIT Jalandhar actively contributes to national missions in energy, sustainability, digital transformation, and advanced manufacturing — themes that closely align with the focus areas of IIPESS 2027.

Places to Visit Nearby

Beyond the campus, both Jalandhar city itself and neighbouring Amritsar offer plenty for visiting participants to explore. Below is a mix of sites right in Jalandhar for a quick visit, plus a few iconic Amritsar landmarks worth a full day trip.

In & Around Jalandhar

~4 km • ~15 min from campus

Devi Talab Mandir

One of Jalandhar's oldest and most revered temples, dedicated to Goddess Durga and built around a sacred tank. A calm, centrally located spot popular with pilgrims and visitors alike.

~6 km • ~15-20 min from campus

St. Mary's Cathedral

Seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Jalandhar, located in the Jalandhar Cantonment. The present cathedral was inaugurated in 1989 on a site with church roots going back to 1847.

~5 km • ~15 min from campus

Jama Masjid, Dargah Imam Nasir

A historic mosque and shrine in the old city, reflecting Jalandhar's centuries-old layered history as a hub of trade and settlement in Punjab.

Day Trip to Amritsar

~80 km • ~1.5 hr from campus

Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest Sikh shrine, Harmandir Sahib, known for its gold-plated sanctum set amid a sacred pool. Open through the night, with the world's largest free community kitchen (langar) serving thousands daily.

~80 km • ~1.5 hr from campus

Jallianwala Bagh, Amritsar

A public garden and national memorial marking the site of the 1919 massacre, a defining moment in India's freedom struggle. Preserved bullet-marked walls and a martyrs' well stand within the grounds. Just a short walk from the Golden Temple.

~110 km • ~2.5 hr from campus

Attari-Wagah Border

India's only road border crossing with Pakistan, famous for its high-energy Beating Retreat ceremony held every evening at sunset, with synchronized drills by the BSF and Pakistan Rangers.

~80 km • ~1.5 hr from campus

Durgiana Temple, Amritsar

A Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Durga, built in a style closely mirroring the Golden Temple and similarly set within its own sacred water tank. Also known as the "Silver Temple" for its ornate silver-plated doors.
