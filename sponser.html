Sponsorship & Partnership

The Department of Electrical Engineering cordially invites esteemed organizations, corporations, startups, and institutions to associate with the International Conference on Intelligent Power, Energy and Sustainable Systems (IIPESS-2027) as valued sponsors, partners, or exhibitors.

IIPESS-2027 aims to serve as a premier platform for fostering innovation, encouraging interdisciplinary dialogue, and bridging the gap between academia and industry. The conference will bring together distinguished researchers, industry experts, and young innovators to explore emerging advancements and real-world applications in the domains of power systems, energy technologies, power electronics, and artificial intelligence.

By partnering or exhibiting with us, your organization will gain prominent visibility among a highly engaged audience, opportunities for meaningful networking, and a chance to contribute to shaping future technological advancements. We offer a range of flexible exhibition, partnership, and sponsorship opportunities tailored to align with your organizational goals and outreach strategies.

We sincerely invite you to be a part of this prestigious event and collaborate with us in making IIPESS-2027 a remarkable success.

Interested organizations are requested to kindly fill out the Google Form to express their interest.
